IO route was associated with a significantly lower return of spontaneous circulation (RR = 0.77, 95% CI: [0.71-0.83]; P < .00001; I2 = 84%), sustained return of spontaneous circulation (RR = 0.87, 95% CI: [0.77-0.97]; P = .02; I2 = 53%) and favorable neurological outcomes at hospital discharge (RR = 0.58, 95% CI: [0.45-0.73]; P < .00001; I2 = 81%) compared to IV route.
