Random effect meta-analysis for stone free rate included data from 36 studies and yielded statistically significant higher stone free rates for LL with a pooled OR = 2.19 (95% confidence interval [CI] = 1.63-2.94, p < 0.001). Random effect meta-analysis for operative time included data from 31 studies and yielded no statistically significant difference in operative time with a pooled MD = -1.09 min (95% CI = -4.39-2. 20, p = 0.5161).
