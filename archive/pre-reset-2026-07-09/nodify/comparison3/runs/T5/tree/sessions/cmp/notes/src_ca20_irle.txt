Reich et al., UC Berkeley IRLE (2024/2025), "Effects of the $20 California Fast-Food Minimum Wage" (AB 1228, effective April 2024).

Key findings (verbatim from IRLE brief):
"an estimated wage increase of 8 to 9 percent for workers covered by the policy"
"no negative effects on fast-food employment"
"price increases of about 1.5 percent— or about 6 cents on a four-dollar hamburger"

Note: Berkeley's evaluation of the largest recent US sectoral experiment (fast food to $20) finds large wage gains, modest (~1.5%) price pass-through, and no significant employment loss; reports no evidence of employers cutting hours or increasing just-in-time scheduling in response.
