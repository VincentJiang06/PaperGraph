# Ashenfelter & Jurajda (2021), "Wages, Minimum Wages, and Price Pass-through: The Case of McDonald's Restaurants", IZA DP No. 14124
URL: https://docs.iza.org/dp14124.pdf

Verbatim quotes (from pdftotext of the PDF):

PASS-THROUGH:
- "Our data imply that McDonald's restaurants pass [through] ..." with "a 0.2 price elasticity with respect to wage increases. Together with the 0.7 (first-stage) elasticity of wage rates with respect to minimum wages, this implies a reduced-form price pass-through of minimum-wage-induced higher costs of labor..."
- "elasticity of hourly wage rates with respect to minimum wages of 0.7."

AUTOMATION (disconfirms the automation-substitution channel):
- "We find no association between the adoption of labor-saving touch screen ordering technology and minimum wage hikes."
- "This evidence is not consistent with technology introduction being a channel for negative employment effects of the minimum wage."
- "In Table 1, we find a precisely estimated zero price coefficient corresponding to this new technology in 2018..."

Interpretation: minimum-wage cost shocks in fast food are passed forward into higher Big Mac prices (cost pass-through), NOT primarily onto workers via kiosk automation — direct evidence against the "employers automate away the jobs" prediction, at least over this period.
