SOURCE: Yicai Global — Richard Koo on China's balance sheet recession (yicaiglobal.com)
VERBATIM QUOTES (Richard Koo):
- "China has a balance sheet problem, probably smaller than Japan's, but you have a big construction problem, and the two together could be quite challenging."
- "Fiscal policy is the key. I will recommend using more fiscal power to revive the economy very strongly."
- "If the government comes in and says, 'We're going to do this fiscal policy, and we're going to keep this fiscal policy until the private sector balance sheets are repaired,' then the private sector will feel very safe."
