SOURCE: China Briefing — "China's Gradual Retirement Age Delay" (china-briefing.com), effective Jan 1, 2025
VERBATIM QUOTES:
- "the statutory retirement age for male employees will gradually be extended from the current 60 years to 63 years."
- Female blue-collar: "from 50 to 55"; female white-collar: "from 55 to 58".
- Men delayed "by one month every four months"; female blue-collar "by one month every two months".
- "Over a period of 15 years" the increases occur, completing by 2040.
