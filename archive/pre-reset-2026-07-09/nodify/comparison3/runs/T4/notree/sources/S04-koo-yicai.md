# S04 — Yicai Global: Richard Koo, "China's Balance Sheet Recession Is Similar, Unlike Japan's"
URL: https://www2.yicaiglobal.com/news/chinas-balance-sheet-recession-is-similar-and-different-to-that-of-japan-nris-richard-koo-says

Verbatim quotes:
- "Once the real estate bubble burst and people realized that they were chasing what I call wrong asset prices, unsustainable asset prices, which was similar in Japan 34 years ago"
- "Everyone in China knows about the balance sheet recession. That's a huge advantage China has over Japan 34 years ago"
- "if the government acts on it, this is a balance sheet recession, the government should be the borrower of last resort, borrow and spend of last resort"
- "in the Chinese case, the construction industry had a huge boom over the years, and it's now 26 percent of China's GDP. Now construction is down very sharply, which means that industry is shrinking very rapidly"
- "Fiscal policy is the key. I will recommend using more fiscal power to revive the economy very strongly"
- "The fact that the Chinese government bond yield is down to 2.4 percent suggests that there are too many savings relative to borrowings"

Line relevance: L6 (balance sheet recession; the stagnation mechanism is DEBT/DEMAND, not demographics per se);
advantage = awareness + fiscal space; risk = construction 26% of GDP shrinking.
