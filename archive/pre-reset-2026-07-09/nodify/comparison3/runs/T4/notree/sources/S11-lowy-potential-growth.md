# S11 — Lowy Institute: "Revising down the rise of China" (potential-growth anchor)
URL: https://www.lowyinstitute.org/publications/revising-down-rise-china

Verbatim (WebFetch-extracted):
- "annual economic growth will slow to about 3% by 2030 and 2% by 2040"
- "growth will slow sharply to roughly 3% a year by 2030 and 2–3% a year on average over the three decades to 2050"
- "China's population is set to shrink and age rapidly over the coming decades"
- "productivity growth has been decelerating, and there are strong reasons to think it will continue to do so"
- "China would overtake the United States to become the world's largest economy in nominal US dollar terms by about 2030"
  but "it would never establish a substantial lead"
- "China would remain far less prosperous and productive per person than America, even by mid-century"
