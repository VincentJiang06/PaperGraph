# S01 — Merics: "Is ageing China facing a Japanese-style 'lost decade'?"
URL: https://merics.org/en/comment/ageing-china-facing-japanese-style-lost-decade

Verbatim quotes:
- "China may not be facing a 'lost decade' akin earlier Japan, but it nonetheless must overcome its own challenges."
- "Although China is old before it is rich, it hence retains convergence growth potential. GDP is still growing at some 6% annually."
- "Japan, on the other hand, was already an advanced industrial economy when its population ageing intensified."
- "China's younger cohort, at least theoretically, are well-positioned to utilize their relative skills to maintain, if not increase, productivity levels."
- "just maintaining output per capita requires improved productivity per capita."
- "There are grounds for hope that, given the right policies, China will not suffer Japan's recent fate. Whether it succeeds or not otherwise, only time will tell."

Line relevance: L1, L2 (convergence room), L7 (calibration: not inevitable, policy-conditional).
