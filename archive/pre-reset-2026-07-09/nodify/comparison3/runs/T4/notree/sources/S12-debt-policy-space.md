Search-summarized (IMF 2024 Article IV):
- "China's general government borrowing rose to an estimated 60.5% of GDP in 2024, while augmented debt (which
  includes LGFVs) has increased to 124% of GDP."
- "the IMF projects that even with fiscal adjustments, augmented general government debt would stabilize only at
  about 150-155 percent of GDP."

Line relevance: L6 ADVERSARIAL to Koo — China's real (augmented) debt ~124% GDP and rising limits the "just do
big fiscal / borrower of last resort" cure; policy space is narrower than the headline central-govt debt implies.
Japan ran deficits with its OWN savings + reserve currency + calmer creditors; China's LGFV overhang is messier.
