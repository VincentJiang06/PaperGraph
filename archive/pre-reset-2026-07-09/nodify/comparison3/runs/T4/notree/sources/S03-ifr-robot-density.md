# S03 — IFR press release: "Robot Density Surges in Europe, Asia, and Americas"
URL: https://ifr.org/ifr-press-releases/news/robot-density-surges-in-europe-asia-and-americas

Verbatim quotes (all-industry robot density metric, 2024 data):
- "It has 166 robots for every 10,000 people employed, which is a year-on-year increase of 17%." (China)
- "China ranks 6th in Asia and 22nd worldwide."
- Republic of Korea: "1,220 robots per 10,000 employees"
- Japan: "446 units"
- Germany: "449 units per 10,000 employees"
- United States: "307 units per 10,000 employees"
- "The country counts around 2 million units — approximately 4.5 times more than Japan, which is in second place." (China operational stock)

NOTE/NUANCE: This is the ALL-EMPLOYED density metric (China 22nd worldwide). The MANUFACTURING-only
density metric (widely cited ~470/10,000, surpassing DE/JP) is a different measure — verify separately (S03b).

Line relevance: L3 (automation).
