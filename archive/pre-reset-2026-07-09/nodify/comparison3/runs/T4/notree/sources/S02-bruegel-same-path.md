# S02 — Bruegel policy brief: "Will China's economy follow the same path as Japan's?"
URL: https://www.bruegel.org/policy-brief/will-chinas-economy-follow-same-path-japans

Verbatim quotes:
- "China now is a poorer country than Japan was then, with still more convergence opportunities ahead."
- "Japan has accumulated public debt very rapidly since the bursting of the bubble, reaching levels above 230 percent of GDP today. China is following the same path."
- "China's real-estate market adjustment has been more rapid than Japan's, raising concerns about a potential 'hard landing.'"
- "Chinese TFP has been declining alongside its economic slowdown...raising doubts about whether these technological breakthroughs will be able to offset continued structural deceleration."
- "It remains unclear – though globally significant – whether China will be able to deviate from the path followed by Japan in the 1980s and 1990s."
- "While China risks following a trajectory that is similar to Japan's, it may be too early for China's policymakers to fully pivot away from its current growth model."
