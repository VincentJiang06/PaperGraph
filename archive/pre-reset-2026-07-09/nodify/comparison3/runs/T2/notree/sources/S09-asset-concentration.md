Verbatim quotes:

- "Most of the wealth of Americans in the bottom 90 percent comes from their homes — the asset category that took the biggest hit during the Great Recession."

Supporting (Fed DFA / Axios, surfaced by WebSearch): top 10% hold roughly 90% of household stock-market wealth.

Relevance: Establishes the MECHANISM the asset-price channel rests on — equity ownership is extremely concentrated at the top, while the bottom 90%'s wealth is predominantly housing. So QE-driven EQUITY gains flow to the top; QE-driven HOUSE-price gains are the main asset transmission to the middle/bottom. Sign of the wealth effect depends on equity-vs-housing relative boost.
