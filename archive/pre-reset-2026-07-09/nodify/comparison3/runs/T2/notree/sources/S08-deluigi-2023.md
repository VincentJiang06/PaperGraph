# S08 — De Luigi, Feldkircher, Poyntner & Schuberth (2023), "Quantitative Easing and Wealth Inequality: The Asset Price Channel", Oxford Bulletin of Economics and Statistics 85(3)
URL: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/obes.12543 (paywalled, 402); findings surfaced verbatim via WebSearch of the abstract.
(ECB QE, 9 euro-area countries, local projections + HFCS micro-simulation.)

Verbatim (abstract text, surfaced by WebSearch):
- "For the majority of the countries under review, expansionary QE via asset prices leads to net wealth inequality increases when measured using wealth indicators that are sensitive to changes at the tails of the wealth distribution."
- "This finding contrasts with results based on the Gini coefficient which point to an equalizing impact of QE."
- "One-third of the households in the sample holds neither housing nor financial wealth and is thus not directly affected by QE measures through the asset prices channel."

Relevance: The MEASURE decides the sign. Tail-sensitive metrics (top-share) => QE raises wealth inequality; the Gini => QE equalizes. ~1/3 of households own no assets at all, so the asset channel bypasses them entirely.
