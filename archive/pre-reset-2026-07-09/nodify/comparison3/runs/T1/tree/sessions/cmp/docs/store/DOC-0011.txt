Responding to High Inflation, with Some Thoughts on a Soft Landing (Gov. Waller speech, 2022-05-30)
URL: https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/waller20220530a.htm

The combination of strong consumer demand and supply constraints—both bottlenecks and a shortage of workers relative to labor demand—is generating very high inflation.

the relationship between vacancies and unemployment gives me reason to hope that policy tightening in current circumstances can tame inflation without causing a sharp increase in unemployment.

reducing vacancies from an extremely high level to a lower (but still strong) level has a relatively limited effect on hiring and on unemployment.
