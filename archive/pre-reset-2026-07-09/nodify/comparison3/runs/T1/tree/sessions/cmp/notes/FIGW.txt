What does the Beveridge curve tell us about the likelihood of a soft landing? (Figura & Waller, Fed FEDS Note 2022)
URL: https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/what-does-the-beveridge-curve-tell-us-about-the-likelihood-of-a-soft-landing-20220729.html

Under most assumptions about current levels of matching efficiency and the curvature of the Beveridge curve, a decline in the vacancy rate from 7 percent to 4.6 percent would lead to an increase in the unemployment rate of about 1 percentage point or less.

quite low and, in our view, consistent with a soft landing.
