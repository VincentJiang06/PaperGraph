Energy commodity prices in 2022 showed effects of Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine (EIA Today in Energy, 2023-01-03)
URL: https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php?id=55059

The WTI crude oil price increased to a monthly average of $114 per barrel in June, the highest price in real terms since September 2014.

After increasing 68% from January through June, the energy component of the S&P Goldman Sachs Commodity Index (GSCI) ended the year 10% higher than the first trading day of 2022.
