# S16 — "The Costless Disinflation of 2022-24" (St. Louis Fed, On the Economy, Aug 2024) + Krugman "Two theories of immaculate disinflation"

URLs:

Verbatim (search-surfaced; both pages 403'd/truncated on direct fetch):
- (Roosevelt/consensus framing) "The majority of disinflation has been driven by expanding supply rather than decreasing demand."
- Krugman: "If the main driver of disinflation was adaptation of the economy's supply side, it's hard to see how higher interest rates did that." — i.e., if supply-driven, the Fed deserves less credit and the soft landing was more "luck"/normalization.

Interpretation: Frames the soft-landing implication — the more of the surge AND the disinflation you attribute to transient supply factors, the more sustainable a non-recessionary landing looks (self-reversing shocks), but the less of it the Fed can claim or repeat.
