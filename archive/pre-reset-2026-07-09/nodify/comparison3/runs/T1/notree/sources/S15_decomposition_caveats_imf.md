# S15 — Method critique: identification limits of supply/demand inflation decompositions (IMF WP 2023/205; Shapiro WP2022-18 caveats; Boston Fed CPP 2022)

URLs:
- https://www.imf.org/-/media/Files/Publications/WP/2023/English/wpiea2023205-print-pdf.ashx
- https://www.frbsf.org/wp-content/uploads/wp2022-18.pdf
- https://www.bostonfed.org/-/media/Documents/Workingpapers/PDF/2022/cpp20221107.pdf

Verbatim (search-surfaced):
- "the structural shocks are only set identified and their size cannot be determined without further identification restrictions."
- "The demand/supply categorization employed is a noisy measure of the underlying economic shocks."
- "the method does not tell us what specific fundamental events or shocks drive movements in prices and quantities."
- "decomposition results can be highly sensitive to the classification of items."

Interpretation: Supports the red-team (L7) that a clean numeric demand-vs-supply split is not point-identified; shares are model- and method-dependent.
