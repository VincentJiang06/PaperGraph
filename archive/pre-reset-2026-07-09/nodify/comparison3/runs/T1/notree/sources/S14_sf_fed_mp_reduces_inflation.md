# S14 — Shapiro, "Does Monetary Policy Tightening Reduce Inflation?" (SF Fed Economic Letter 2025-04, Feb 2025)

URL: https://www.frbsf.org/research-and-insights/publications/economic-letter/2025/02/does-monetary-policy-tightening-reduce-inflation/

Verbatim quotes (fetched):
- "contributions to core inflation from demand declining 2 percentage points since the summer of 2022" (search version: "the largest decline for any deliberate disinflation attempt since 1969")
- "the demand-driven contribution to core PCE inflation falls 0.5 percentage point over a 12-month period" (in response to a 0.25 percentage point interest rate increase)
- "if the FOMC had not raised interest rates... demand-driven inflation would have continued rising into 2023 instead of declining as it did"

Interpretation: Counters S13/Roosevelt — argues the Fed materially reduced the DEMAND-driven component, i.e., demand was a real and policy-addressable driver.
