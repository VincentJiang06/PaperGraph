# S12 — "Supply-Side Expansion Has Driven the Decline in Inflation" (Roosevelt Institute)

URL: https://rooseveltinstitute.org/publications/supply-side-expansion-has-driven-the-decline-in-inflation/

Verbatim quotes (fetched):
- "The majority of disinflation has been driven by expanding supply rather than decreasing demand."
- "73 percent of all core items, and 66 percent of services, see prices falling with quantities increasing—a sign of expanding supply."
- "Of the core PCE categories that saw price declines, weighting by their share of nominal consumption, we see that 73 percent came from supply expansion, while 27 percent came from decreasing demand."
- "Supply increasing as the driver of disinflation is very notable for core goods, where 87 percent is driven by an expansion of supply."
- "66 percent of the decrease in services inflation has ended up with increasing quantities."
