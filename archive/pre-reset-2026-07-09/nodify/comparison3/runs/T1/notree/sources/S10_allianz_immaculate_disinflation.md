# S10 — "US immaculate disinflation: How much should we thank the Fed for?" (Allianz Research, 2024)

URL: https://www.allianz.com/en/economic_research/insights/publications/specials_fmo/us-inflation.html

Verbatim quotes (fetched):
- "the Fed has contributed to lowering inflation through two channels: (i) by cooling aggregate demand growth (-2pps) and (ii) by managing to keep inflation expectations anchored (-3pps). In total, the Fed has pulled inflation down by -5pps over the past 12 months."
- "The fading of supply-chain disruptions contributed equally to lower inflation... this has contributed to reducing US inflation by -5pps."
- "US inflation has fallen by -7pps over the past year... the bulk of the easing stems from the end of supply-chain disruptions (-5pp), with relatively minor contributions from other factors (lower oil prices: -0.6pp; lower inflation expectations: -0.3pp)."
- "Half of the decline in US inflation since last year is the making of the Fed."

NOTE: The piece is internally in tension — it both credits the Fed with ~half (largely via the contested "expectations anchoring" channel) AND says "the bulk of the easing stems from the end of supply-chain disruptions." Good illustration of attribution being model-dependent.
