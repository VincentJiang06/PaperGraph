Verbatim quotes (fetched):

- "The labor market had little effect on inflation early on, but increased tightness eventually produced limited but sustained pressure on inflation."

From the search-surfaced abstract:
- "the U.S. inflation... was initially the result of a series of adverse relative price shocks and sectoral shortages, each of which had a strong but largely transient effect on inflation."
- "Most of the inflation surge that began in 2021 was the result of shocks to prices given wages, contrary to earlier concerns about labor market overheating."
- "as in the United States, pandemic-era inflation was due primarily to supply disruptions and sharp increases in the prices of food and energy; however, the inflationary effects of these supply shocks have not been persistent, in part due to the credibility of central bank inflation targets."
