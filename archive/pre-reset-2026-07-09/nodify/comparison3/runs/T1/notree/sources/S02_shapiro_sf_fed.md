# S02 — Adam Shapiro, "Decomposing Supply and Demand Driven Inflation" + SF Fed Supply-/Demand-Driven PCE series

URLs:
- https://www.frbsf.org/wp-content/uploads/wp2022-18.pdf
- https://www.frbsf.org/research-and-insights/data-and-indicators/supply-and-demand-driven-pce-inflation/

Method (verbatim, fetched from data page / FEDS 2025 abstract):
- "Demand-driven categories are identified as those where an unexpected change in price moves in the same direction as the change in quantity in a given month. Supply-driven categories are identified as those where unexpected changes in price and quantity move in opposite directions."

Caveats to method (verbatim, search-surfaced from Shapiro WP2022-18 / IMF WP2023/205):
- "A caveat to the identification scheme is that it includes the endogenous reactions to aggregate supply and demand shocks. For example, a negative oil supply shock can cause a positive demand shock for non-oil fuels."
- "While sectors are categorized in a quarter as either demand or supply driven, in reality supply and demand shocks will simultaneously affect a sector."
- "the method does not tell us what specific fundamental events or shocks drive movements in prices and quantities."
