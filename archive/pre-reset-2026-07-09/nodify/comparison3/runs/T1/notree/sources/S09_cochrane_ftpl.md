# S09 — Cochrane (& Coleman), Fiscal Theory of the Price Level applied to 2021 inflation (CFA Institute Enterprising Investor, 2022)

URL: https://rpc.cfainstitute.org/blogs/enterprising-investor/2022/cochrane-and-coleman-the-fiscal-theory-of-the-price-level-and-inflation-episodes

Verbatim quotes (fetched):
- "The US government printed up about $3 trillion of money and sent people checks. It borrowed an additional $2 trillion of money and sent people more checks."
- "Sending people checks is a particularly powerful way of getting them to spend the new debt rather than hold it as savings."
- "We couldn't have asked for a cleaner exercise for chapter one of fiscal theory of the price level in how to create inflation."
- "Historically, all significant inflations have come from fiscal problems."

Note (search): Cochrane concedes "The COVID crisis was a supply shock if there ever was one" — his claim is that fiscal expansion turned that into inflation.
