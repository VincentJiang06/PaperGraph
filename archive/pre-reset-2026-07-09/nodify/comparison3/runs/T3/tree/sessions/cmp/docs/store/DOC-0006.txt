SOURCE: Brynjolfsson, Li, Raymond (2023). "Generative AI at Work." NBER WP 31161 (Apr 2023); QJE 140(2), 2025. Field deployment across 5,179 customer-support agents.
URL: https://www.nber.org/papers/w31161

VERBATIM (abstract):
"Access to the tool increases productivity, as measured by issues resolved per hour, by 14% on average, including a 34% improvement for novice and low-skilled workers but with minimal impact on experienced and highly skilled workers."

DISTILLED: Causal field evidence that generative AI at task level is AUGMENTING (esp. novices), raising throughput and quality — a complementarity/productivity channel that can offset displacement and blunt net job loss. Same occupation (customer service) that later shows entry-level decline (doc03): augmentation and displacement coexist.
