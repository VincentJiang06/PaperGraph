VERBATIM:
"agents in Mid 2025 are 'impressive in theory but in practice unreliable.'"
"50% success horizon for frontier models at ~50 minutes on human expert tasks" (METR, Jul 14 2025)
"models scoring 70%+ on standard SWE-bench drop to ~23% on long-horizon tasks involving multi-file refactors and cross-repository changes" (Scale AI SWE-Bench Pro, Sep 19 2025)
τ-bench: "GPT-4o achieves 61% pass@1 but 25% pass@8 on retail agent tasks" (consistency across attempts collapses)

DISTILLED: Through 2025, autonomous agents remained unreliable on long-horizon, multi-step real tasks; success horizon ~tens of minutes, and consistency (pass^k) far below single-attempt. Supports skeptic view: broad task EXPOSURE far exceeds reliable end-to-end substitution — capability, not just diffusion, gates realized job displacement.
