DISTILLED: Real usage-side data show augmentation (~57%) > automation (~43%); usage concentrated in coding/writing. Later reports (Nov 2025) put augmentation 52% vs automation 45%. Automation dominates programmatic API traffic. Interpretation: realized use is augmentation-leaning, not wholesale job automation.
