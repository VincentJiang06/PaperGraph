# MIT Project NANDA — "The GenAI Divide: State of AI in Business 2025" (Aug 2025)
URLs:
- https://fortune.com/2025/08/18/mit-report-95-percent-generative-ai-pilots-at-companies-failing-cfo/
- https://mlq.ai/media/quarterly_decks/v0.1_State_of_AI_in_Business_2025_Report.pdf

KEY FINDING:
- Despite $30-40B enterprise investment, ~95% of enterprise generative-AI pilots delivered NO measurable P&L return; only ~5% cross the "GenAI Divide."
- Failure blamed on implementation/integration approach, not model quality.
- Biggest realized ROI = back-office automation (eliminating BPO / agency spend); buying from vendors succeeds ~67% vs internal builds ~1/3 as often.
- Method: 150 interviews, 350-employee survey, 300 public deployments; research Jan-Jun 2025.

Relevance: direct evidence that productive/organizational diffusion LAGS usage diffusion => supports muted near-term labor effect AND a pending lag once integration matures.
