# World Economic Forum — Future of Jobs Report 2025 (Jan 2025)
URL: https://www.weforum.org/press/2025/01/future-of-jobs-report-2025-78-million-new-job-opportunities-by-2030-but-urgent-upskilling-needed-to-prepare-workforces/
Report PDF: https://reports.weforum.org/docs/WEF_Future_of_Jobs_Report_2025.pdf

KEY FIGURES (employer survey, ~1,000 firms / 14M workers / 55 economies):
- By 2030: 170 million new roles CREATED, 92 million DISPLACED => NET +78 million jobs (~+7% of today's total employment). Total churn = 22% of jobs.
- Fastest-growing: AI/big-data specialists, FinTech, software. Fastest-declining: clerical/administrative (cashiers, admin assistants, data-entry).
- "nearly 40% of skills required on the job set to change" by 2030; 63% of employers cite skills gap as key barrier.

Interpretation: a JOB-LEVEL forecast that net effect is POSITIVE, but with large gross displacement + reallocation. Employer perceptions, not realized data.
