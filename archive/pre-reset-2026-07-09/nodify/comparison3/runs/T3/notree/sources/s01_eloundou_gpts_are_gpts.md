# Eloundou, Manning, Mishkin, Rock — "GPTs are GPTs: An Early Look at the Labor Market Impact Potential of Large Language Models" (2023/2024)
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.10130 (also Science 2024, doi:10.1126/science.adj0998)

VERBATIM (abstract):
- "around 80% of the U.S. workforce could have at least 10% of their work tasks affected by the introduction of LLMs, while approximately 19% of workers may see at least 50% of their tasks impacted."
- "When incorporating software and tooling built on top of LLMs, this share increases to between 47 and 56% of all tasks."
- "We do not make predictions about the development or adoption timeline of such LLMs." (i.e., figures = potential EXPOSURE, not realized automation)

Note: "exposure" defined as tasks where LLM use could reduce time to complete by >=50% at equal quality — a measure of POTENTIAL, explicitly not a forecast of displacement.
