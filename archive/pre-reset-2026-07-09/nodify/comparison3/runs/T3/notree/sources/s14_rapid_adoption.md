# Bick, Blandin, Deming — "The Rapid Adoption of Generative AI" (NBER 32966, 2024) + coverage
URLs:
- https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w32966/w32966.pdf
- https://news.harvard.edu/gazette/story/2024/10/generative-ai-embraced-faster-than-internet-pcs/
- https://aibusiness.com/nlp/ubs-chatgpt-is-the-fastest-growing-app-of-all-time

KEY FIGURES (as reported):
- Two years after ChatGPT, ~39% of individuals reported using generative AI (32% in the prior week) vs ~20% for PCs three years post-IBM-PC and ~20% for the internet two years post-commercialization.
- "Generative AI reached 53% adoption ... within three years of ChatGPT's launch" — faster than PC or internet at comparable horizons.
- UBS: ChatGPT = fastest-growing consumer app (100M users in ~2 months). "In twenty years following the Internet space, we cannot recall a faster ramp in a consumer internet app."

DOUBLE-EDGED for the lag debate:
- Cuts AGAINST a long lag: usage/access diffusion is unprecedentedly fast, so any labor effect need not wait decades.
- But (with s12 MIT + s05 J-curve) the gap between USAGE diffusion and PRODUCTIVE/organizational diffusion remains; effect on jobs can still lag even as adoption races.
