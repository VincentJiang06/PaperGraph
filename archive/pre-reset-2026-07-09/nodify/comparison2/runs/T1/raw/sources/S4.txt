=== EXTRACTED CONTENT / VERBATIM QUOTES ===

Worker Exposure to LLMs:
"around 80% of the U.S. workforce could have at least 10% of their work tasks affected" and "approximately 19% of workers may see at least 50% of their tasks impacted."

Task Completion Speed:
"about 15% of all worker tasks in the US could be completed significantly faster at the same level of quality" with LLM access alone; "between 47 and 56% of all tasks" when incorporating LLM-powered software and tools.

Definition of Exposure:
The research assesses occupations by examining "their alignment with LLM capabilities" using a new rubric that integrates "both human expertise and GPT-4 classifications." A task is exposed if access to an LLM (or LLM-powered software) can reduce the time required to complete it by at least 50% while maintaining quality.

Additional Context:
"higher-income jobs potentially facing greater exposure" and LLMs "exhibit traits of general-purpose technologies," suggesting broad economic implications across wage levels and industries.
