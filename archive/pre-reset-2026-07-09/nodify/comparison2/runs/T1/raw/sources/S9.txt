=== EXTRACTED CONTENT / VERBATIM QUOTES ===

Overall Productivity Impact:
"Access to the tool increases productivity, as measured by issues resolved per hour, by 14% on average."

Heterogeneous Effects by Worker Experience:
The study found a "34% improvement for novice and low-skilled workers but with minimal impact on experienced and highly skilled workers."

Mechanism:
The researchers offer evidence suggesting "the AI model disseminates the best practices of more able workers and helps newer workers move down the experience curve."

Additional Benefits:
Beyond productivity, the research indicates the intervention "improves customer sentiment, increases employee retention, and may lead to worker learning."

=== STUDY DESIGN (from WebSearch context) ===
Staggered introduction of a generative AI-based conversational assistant, studied using data from 5,179 customer support agents at a Fortune 500 software firm; the AI tool read customer chats and suggested responses to agents in real time.
