=== EXTRACTED CONTENT / VERBATIM QUOTES ===

Highest Usage Occupations:
"37.2% of queries sent to Claude were in this category, covering tasks like software modification, code debugging, and network troubleshooting" (Computer & Mathematical roles). The second-largest was Arts/Media at "10.3% of queries" focused on writing and editing.

Augmentation vs. Automation Split:
"Overall, we saw a slight lean towards augmentation, with 57% of tasks being augmented and 43% of tasks being automated."

Employment Implications:
The analysis explicitly avoids direct employment predictions: "As we predicted, there wasn't evidence in this dataset of jobs being entirely automated: instead, AI was diffused across the many tasks in the economy." They note that "roughly 36% of jobs had some use of AI for at least 25% of their tasks" while "only approximately 4% of jobs used AI for at least 75% of tasks."

=== ADDITIONAL CONTEXT FROM SEARCH (later Anthropic Economic Index reports, WebSearch) ===
In a later report (pooling data across reports through ~2026), the share of jobs where Claude is used for at least a quarter of tasks rose from 36% (Jan 2025 baseline) to 49%. On Claude.ai specifically, augmentation (52% of conversations) has overtaken automation (45%) as the most common interaction pattern; but when businesses embed Claude into applications via the API, 77% of API transcripts show automation patterns versus just 12% for augmentation — i.e., automation is far more common in production/API deployments than in direct chat use.
