Title: AI and Job Postings: From Destruction to Creation?
Publisher: Indeed Hiring Lab
Date: 2026-07-08
URL: https://www.hiringlab.org/2026/07/08/ai-and-job-postings-from-destruction-to-creation/
Accessed via: WebFetch, 2026-07-09

Verbatim key quotes:

"the more exposed to AI an occupation is, the more it declined" (job postings, May 2022 to May 2026)

"the more exposed to AI an occupation is, on average, the more it rebounded" (since 2025) — described as a reversal from destruction to creation.

On the software-development rebound coinciding with the launch of Claude Code (late Feb 2025): postings "has risen almost 15%, while job postings overall have declined by 7%" over the same window.

"71% of the increase in Software Development job postings between May 2025 and May 2026 came from senior roles, and 37% came from jobs that mention AI in their title."

"even after the recent rise, software development job postings remain about 27.5% below their pre-pandemic level."

Causal caveat (authors' own words): "While correlation does not imply causation, the introduction of Claude Code...is a coincidence that cannot be ignored," while acknowledging "many other factors influencing the market" and describing the finding as "preliminary evidence."

Notes: Directly relevant to the task-vs-job distinction: even in an occupation (software development) with very high AI task-exposure, job postings first fell sharply, then partially rebounded — but the rebound is concentrated in senior/AI-adjacent roles, not a simple return to junior hiring. This complicates a simple "task exposure -> job loss" reading and also complicates simple "AI creates net new jobs" optimism, since junior-level postings remain depressed.
