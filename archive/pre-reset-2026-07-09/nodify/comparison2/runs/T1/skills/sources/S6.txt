Title: 2025 Year-End Challenger Report: Highest Q4 Layoffs Since 2008; Lowest YTD Hiring Since 2010
Publisher: Challenger, Gray & Christmas, Inc.
URL: https://www.challengergray.com/blog/2025-year-end-challenger-report-highest-q4-layoffs-since-2008-lowest-ytd-hiring-since-2010/
Accessed via: WebFetch, 2026-07-09

Verbatim key quotes:

"In 2025, employers announced 1,206,374 job cuts, an increase of 58% from the 761,358 announced in 2024."

"Artificial Intelligence (AI) was cited for 142 job cuts in December. So far this year, AI has been responsible for 54,836 announced layoff plans."

"Last year, U.S. employers announced 507,647 planned hires, down 34% from the 769,953 announced in 2024. It is the lowest year-to-date total since 2010, when 402,608 new hires were planned that year."

"Technology has been pivoting to both developing and implementing artificial intelligence much more quickly than any other industry. This coupled with over-hiring over the last decade created a wave of job loss in the industry."

Notes: AI-cited layoffs (~54,836) are a small fraction (~4.5%) of total 2025 announced job cuts (1,206,374), and Challenger's own commentary attributes much of tech-sector job loss to a combination of AI adoption AND prior over-hiring correction — i.e., AI is one of several confounded causes, not cleanly separable in this data.
