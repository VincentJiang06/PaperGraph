Title: MIT report: 95% of generative AI pilots at companies are failing
Publication: Fortune (covering MIT NANDA "The GenAI Divide: State of AI in Business 2025" report)
URL: https://fortune.com/2025/08/18/mit-report-95-percent-generative-ai-pilots-at-companies-failing-cfo/
Accessed via: WebFetch, 2026-07-09

Verbatim key quotes:

"The 95% failure rate for enterprise AI solutions represents the clearest manifestation of the GenAI Divide"

"about 5% of AI pilot programs achieve rapid revenue acceleration; the vast majority stall"

"Rather than mass layoffs, companies are increasingly not backfilling positions as they become vacant."

Disruption noted as concentrated "in customer support and administrative roles, particularly positions previously outsourced."

Methodology (from underlying MIT NANDA report, as summarized): based on 52 executive interviews, 153 leader surveys, and analysis of 300 public AI deployments.

Notes: This source is important for the "adoption friction" mechanism: even where firms adopt genAI/agents, most pilots fail to reach production value, and the observed labor-market adjustment channel is disproportionately "quiet attrition" (not backfilling vacancies) rather than visible mass layoffs — which would make AI's net employment effect systematically under-visible in headline layoff statistics.
