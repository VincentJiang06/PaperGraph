Source: The Robot Report, "In just seven years, the global robot density in factories has doubled, IFR finds" (reporting IFR World Robotics 2024)
URL: https://www.therobotreport.com/in-just-seven-years-global-robot-density-factories-has-doubled-ifr-finds/

Key extracted figures:
"China reached a high robot density of 470 robots per 10,000 employees, compared with 402 units in 2022."
China achieved third place worldwide in 2023, surpassing both Germany and Japan for the first time in this metric.
Comparative density figures (2023): China 470 units; Germany 429 units; Japan 419 units; South Korea 1,012 units.
"China's massive investment in automation technology has achieved this high robot density despite a huge manufacturing workforce of around 37 million people."
China increased robot density by 68 units per 10,000 employees in a single year (2022->2023), but still significantly lags South Korea's density level.

Separately, per IFR/Businesswire coverage of World Robotics 2024/2025: 54% of all robots installed worldwide in 2024 were deployed in China (295,000 units); China has the largest operational stock of industrial robots in the world, around 2 million units, approximately 4.5 times more than Japan (second place).
