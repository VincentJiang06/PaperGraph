Title: The Effects on Employment and Family Income of Increasing the Federal Minimum Wage (analysis of the Raise the Wage Act's $15 federal minimum wage by 2025)
Author/Publisher: Congressional Budget Office (CBO), July 2019
URL (news coverage synthesizing the report): https://www.npr.org/2019/07/08/739607964/-15-minimum-wage-would-boost-17-million-workers-cut-1-3-million-jobs-cbo-says ; https://www.cnbc.com/2019/07/08/cbo-estimates-effects-of-house-democrats-15-minimum-wage-plan.html

Verbatim / closely-quoted key figures (as reported):
"1.3 million people would lose jobs if the federal minimum wage were raised to $15 per hour by 2025" (median estimate)
"resulting job losses could range between 'about zero and 3.7 million' depending on various assumptions."
Also: raising the federal minimum wage to $15/hour by 2025 would raise pay for over 27 million (later reporting: 17 million directly) workers and lift 1.3 million people out of poverty.
