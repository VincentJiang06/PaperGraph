**Affected Demographics:**
"The drop in employment would be felt most strongly by the youngest and least educated workers."

"Employers may choose to shift spending toward employing fewer higher-wage workers or investing in technology at the expense of low-wage workers."

(Cross-checked against WebSearch summary of the original CBO report: CBO's average estimate was that employment would be reduced by 1.4 million workers, or 0.9 percent, by 2025 when the federal minimum wage would reach $15; roughly half of displaced workers were projected to leave the labor force entirely; 17 million workers would be directly affected; poverty would fall by 0.9 million people; the 10-year budget deficit would rise by $54 billion.)
