**Abstract Summary:**
The researchers examined California's $20 fast food minimum wage enacted in September 2023 and implemented April 2024 (AB 1228).

**Key Verbatim Findings:**
1. "employment in California's fast food sector declined by 2.7 percent relative to employment in the fast food sector elsewhere"
2. "Adjusting for pre-AB 1228 trends increases this differential decline to 3.2 percent"
3. "Our median estimate translates into a loss of 18,000 jobs in California's fast food sector"

**Study Details:**
The analysis used Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages data covering September 2023 through September 2024. The researchers applied multiple methodologies, including trend adjustments and comparisons with non-minimum-wage-intensive industries, with each adjustment showing larger employment declines than the previous measure.
