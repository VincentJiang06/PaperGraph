Title: Speech by Chair Powell on monetary policy and price stability (Jackson Hole, August 26, 2022)
Publisher: Federal Reserve Board
URL: https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/powell20220826a.htm
Fetched: 2026-07-09 (via WebSearch summary)

Extracted content:

Restoring price stability requires "using tools forcefully to bring demand and supply into better balance."

Powell stated the Fed was moving its policy stance purposefully to a level that would be "sufficiently restrictive to return inflation to 2 percent," and that restoring price stability would likely require "maintaining a restrictive policy stance for some time," with "the historical record cautioning strongly against prematurely loosening policy."

The Fed was taking "forceful and rapid steps to moderate demand so that it comes into better alignment with supply, and to keep inflation expectations anchored" — likely resulting in "some weakening of the US economy and job market," with higher rates, slower growth and softer labor conditions bringing "pain to households and businesses," but framed as preferable to the greater pain of failing to restore price stability (citing lessons of the 1970s-80s).
