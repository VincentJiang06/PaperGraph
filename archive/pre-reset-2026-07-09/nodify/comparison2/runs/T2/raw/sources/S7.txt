Title: Is the American Rescue Plan Taking Us Back to the '60s? (FRBSF Economic Letter 2021-27)
Publisher: Federal Reserve Bank of San Francisco
URL: https://www.frbsf.org/research-and-insights/publications/economic-letter/2021/10/is-american-rescue-plan-taking-us-back-to-1960s/
Fetched: 2026-07-09 (via WebFetch)

Extracted verbatim quotes:

"the fiscal plan acts to temporarily raise the vacancy-to-unemployment ratio, in turn pushing up inflation by about 0.3 percentage point per year through 2022."

The ARP "is expected to cause inflation to increase by about 0.3 percentage point in 2021 and by a bit more than 0.2 percentage point in 2022," with "negligible" impact in 2023.

The vacancy-to-unemployment ratio was projected to rise "approximately 0.6 units over 2021, and less than 0.5 units by the end of 2022" due to the ARP.

Overall assessment: the estimated ARP inflation impact represents "meaningful" but temporary inflationary pressure — "a far cry from the strong overheating of the 1960s."
