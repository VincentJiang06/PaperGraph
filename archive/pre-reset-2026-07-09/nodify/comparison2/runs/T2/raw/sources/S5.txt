Fetched: 2026-07-09 (via WebFetch)

Extracted verbatim quotes:

"I think 90 percent of inflation is the result of excess demand driven by fiscal and monetary policy." — Jason Furman

"To think $2.5 trillion in fiscal stimulus — amounting to 11 percent of GDP — would not cause inflation" required believing either that such a huge adjustment was possible within a matter of months or that fiscal policy is ineffective and does not increase aggregate demand.

Furman noted that fiscal multipliers indicated 2021 spending would rise significantly while "production constraints suggested output would not increase by as much. The difference was unexpectedly higher inflation."
