Title: Larry Summers sends inflation warning to White House: Dominant risk to economy is 'overheating'
Publisher: ABC17NEWS (citing CNN interview), National Politics wire
URL: https://abc17news.com/politics/national-politics/2021/05/12/larry-summers-sends-inflation-warning-to-white-house-dominant-risk-to-economy-is-overheating/
Date: May 12, 2021

Key verbatim extracted content (Larry Summers, on the $1.9 trillion American Rescue Plan):

"the risk of a Vietnam inflation scenario is now greater than the deflation risks on which they [Fed/White House] were originally focused"

"overheating — not excess slack — is the dominant economic risk facing the US over the next year or two"

"My concerns about overheating have grown substantially over the last several months"

Context: Summers had previously warned Biden's $1.9 trillion COVID relief bill might overstimulate the economy by sparking excessive inflation; the White House disputed this assessment at the time (May 2021).
