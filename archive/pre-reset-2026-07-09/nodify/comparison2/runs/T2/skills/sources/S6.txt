Title: Stimulus Spending a Factor, But Far From Whole Story on Inflation
Publisher: FactCheck.org
URL: https://www.factcheck.org/2022/06/stimulus-spending-a-factor-but-far-from-whole-story-on-inflation/
Date: June 2022

Key verbatim extracted content (economist estimates of ARP's inflation contribution, out of ~8.5% headline CPI):

Jason Furman (Harvard, former Obama economic adviser): "his estimate is 1 to 4 percentage points and when 'pressed for one number,' he uses the midpoint of 2.5."

George Selgin (Cato Institute): "a 'good guess might be 2 to 3' percentage points."

San Francisco Federal Reserve: a separate SF Fed estimate suggested the fiscal-support impact could be around 3 percentage points by Q4 2021 (distinct from the SF Fed's own published 0.3pp/year estimate in the Oct 2021 Economic Letter — see S7; the article notes considerable estimate dispersion across sources).

Mark Zandi (Moody's Analytics): "the ARP contributed only 0.1 percentage points to year-over-year inflation in May" 2022 — i.e. the fiscal impulse had largely faded out by then.

Editorial framing: "The $1.9 trillion American Rescue Plan helped fuel about half or less of the increase, analysts say, with other causes of inflation including COVID-19's impact on labor markets and supply chains." The piece stresses considerable uncertainty and disagreement among economists on the precise magnitude.
