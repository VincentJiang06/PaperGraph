Reporting on Jason Furman's (former CEA chair under Obama) public estimate of the American Rescue Plan's inflationary impact, as summarized by PolitiFact ("Biden's American Rescue Plan fueled inflation. So did post-COVID shortages," April 2022) and ABC News ("Republicans at RNC blame Biden for inflation. Economists say it's misleading").

Jason Furman estimated that Biden's American Rescue Plan added between 1 percentage point and 4 percentage points to the inflation rate in 2021. Some economists estimate it added two percentage points, some say up to four percentage points -- accounting for something between one quarter to one half of the 8.5% inflation rate recorded in March 2022. This contrasts with the San Francisco Fed's more modest estimate of about 0.3 percentage point per year through 2022, illustrating substantial model-dependent disagreement in fiscal-contribution estimates.
