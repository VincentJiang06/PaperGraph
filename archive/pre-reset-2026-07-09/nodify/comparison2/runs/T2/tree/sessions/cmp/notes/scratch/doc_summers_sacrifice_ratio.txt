Reporting and commentary on Lawrence Summers's "sacrifice ratio" argument: Slate, "Why Larry Summers thinks we need massive unemployment to beat inflation" (July 2022); The American Prospect, "Why Were Inflation Hawks Wrong?" (Feb 2024).

Summers estimated that the sacrifice ratio was two percentage points of unemployment (sustained over a year) per point of inflation reduction, implying that cutting inflation by roughly 2.5 points would require a large, sustained rise in unemployment (historically consistent with recession). By early 2023 the data revealed that CPI decelerated substantially while unemployment remained low, an outcome inconsistent with the standard Phillips-curve/sacrifice-ratio prediction. In July 2023, Summers himself acknowledged: "given how hot the economy is -- the inflation performance at this point is better than I think many standard models would have predicted."
