FRBSF Economic Letter 2023-16, "Global Supply Chain Pressures and U.S. Inflation" (San Francisco Fed, June 2023).

Using the NY Fed's Global Supply Chain Pressure Index (GSCPI), the analysis finds: "Supply chain pressures...accounted for about 60% of the surge in U.S. inflation beginning in early 2021." Further: "GSCPI shocks contributed on average about 60% of the above-trend run-up of headline inflation" during April 2021 to March 2023. On the decline: "Since mid-2022, the contributions of GSCPI shocks have declined from 2.5 to 1.4 percentage points, and headline inflation has also slowed." A one-standard-deviation shock to the GSCPI (estimated on the 1998-2019 pre-pandemic period, controlling for unemployment and monetary-policy conditions) leads to an increase in headline PCE inflation of about 0.5 percentage points.
