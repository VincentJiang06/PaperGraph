FRBSF Economic Letter 2021-27, "Is the American Rescue Plan Taking Us Back to the '60s?" (San Francisco Fed, October 2021).

Estimates that the fiscal plan acts to temporarily raise the vacancy-to-unemployment ratio, in turn pushing up inflation. Quote: "the ARP is projected to cause a transitory increase in the vacancy-to-unemployment ratio, which translates into a core inflation rate that is about 0.3 percentage point higher per year through 2022."
