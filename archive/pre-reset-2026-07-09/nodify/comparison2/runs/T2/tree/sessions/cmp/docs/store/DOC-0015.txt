CEPR VoxEU column, "Navigating the 2022 inflation surge: Lessons for monetary policy frameworks."

Core argument: conventional monetary policy has inherent limitations when inflation stems from supply disruptions: "rate hikes cannot resolve disrupted supply chains or restore grain exports from a war zone." The 2022 experience showed that inflation-targeting and non-targeting central banks alike had broadly similar inflation outcomes despite inflation-targeters raising rates more aggressively -- "inflation outcomes were broadly similar" across regimes. Central insight: "when price pressures are externally driven, even the most credible communication cannot offset reality." The piece recommends central banks acknowledge the limits of monetary tools transparently, coordinate with fiscal/structural policy, and avoid rigidly defending inflation targets through aggressive tightening, warning that overly aggressive tightening "risks unnecessary economic damage without improving inflation outcomes."
