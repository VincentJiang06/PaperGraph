Bick, Blandin & Deming — The Rapid Adoption of Generative AI. NBER Working Paper 32966 (2024/2025); Management Science (2025). https://www.nber.org/papers/w32966

VERBATIM KEY FINDINGS (from abstract / NBER digest):

"nearly 40 percent of the U.S. population age 18-64 uses generative AI"

"23 percent of employed respondents had used generative AI for work at least once" in the previous week, and "9 percent used it every work day".

"Between 1 and 5 percent of all work hours are currently assisted by generative AI, and respondents report time savings equivalent to 1.4 percent of total work hours."

"Relative to each technology's first mass-market product launch, work adoption of generative AI has been as fast as the personal computer (PC), and overall adoption has been faster than either PCs or the internet."

Survey design: a series of nationally representative U.S. surveys of generative AI use at work and at home, fielded August and November 2024, collectively more than 10,000 respondents.
