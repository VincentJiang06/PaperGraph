Azar, J., Gine, M. & Sanz-Espin, J. (2025, SSRN 5842084, Dec 2025). AI Is Already Eroding Wages: Quasi-Experimental Evidence From Occupational Exposure.
Data/design: large-scale dataset of realized worker-firm matches, 138 million U.S. workers; quasi-experimental, occupational exposure to automation as identification; event around arrival of ChatGPT.
Verbatim (SSRN summary): "the arrival of ChatGPT significantly reduced the wages of firms whose occupational structure was more exposed to automation relative to less exposed firms."
"The average wage of exposed firms fell 4.5% relative to unexposed firms."
"starting wages of new positions for junior and mid-level workers in exposed firms fell by 6.3% and 5.9% respectively relative to unexposed firms, while senior wages remained stable or even increased."
"Firms in exposed sectors reduced the share of junior employees and increased the share of mid-level employees."
Also: exposed firms reduced the level of education required for new junior/mid-level positions.
