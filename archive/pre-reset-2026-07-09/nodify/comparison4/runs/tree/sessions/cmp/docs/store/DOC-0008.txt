Humlum & Vestergaard — "Still Waters, Rapid Currents: Early Labor Market Transformation under Generative AI" (previously circulated as "Large Language Models, Small Labor Market Effects") — NBER Working Paper 33777 (May 2025, revised March 2026).
KEY RESULT (verbatim, paper body): We find precisely null effects of AI chatbot use on earnings and hours. Difference-in-differences estimates for earnings, recorded hours, and wages all center on zero, with confidence intervals ruling out average effects larger than 2%. These null results hold even among workers who use chatbots daily, report substantial productivity gains, perform new AI-related tasks, or work in firms that encourage and invest in chatbots. Moreover, we find no significant effects in any of our 11 occupations, including those with flexible, decentralized wage-setting.
CONTEXT: Links two large-scale adoption surveys (late 2023 and 2024) to Danish administrative labor records, covering ~25,000 workers and ~7,000 workplaces across 11 AI-exposed occupations. Users report average time savings of ~2.8% of work hours, contrasting with the >15% productivity gains documented by RCTs.
DESIGN: difference-in-differences on population administrative data linked to adoption surveys (high methodological tier; precise nulls). LIMITS: Denmark only; 2023-2024 window (early diffusion); measures earnings/hours, not output/productivity.
