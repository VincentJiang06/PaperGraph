Acemoglu, D. & Restrepo, P. (2022). Tasks, Automation, and the Rise in U.S. Wage Inequality. Econometrica 90(5): 1973-2016. (NBER w28920, 2021.)
Design: task-based framework linking wage changes of a demographic group to the task displacement it experiences; shift-share style decomposition; US 1980-2016.
Verbatim (abstract): "Between 50% and 70% of changes in the U.S. wage structure over the last four decades are accounted for by relative wage declines of worker groups specialized in routine tasks in industries experiencing rapid automation."
Mechanism: automation technologies expand the set of tasks performed by capital, displacing worker groups from tasks for which they have comparative advantage, lowering their relative wages.
