Gmyrek, Berg & Bescond (ILO) — "Generative AI and Jobs: A Global Analysis of Potential Effects on Job Quantity and Quality" — ILO Working Paper 96 (August 2023).
KEY RESULTS (verbatim): the overwhelming effect of the technology will be to augment occupations, rather than to automate them. Only the broad occupation of clerical work is highly exposed to the technology with 24 percent of clerical tasks considered highly exposed and an additional 58 percent with medium-level exposure, while for other occupational groups, the greatest share of highly exposed tasks oscillates between 1 and 4 percent.
CONTEXT: Uses GPT-4 to score task-level potential exposure across occupations; computes employment effects at the global level and by country income group; exposure rises with country income level and differs by gender (clerical is female-dominated).
DESIGN: potential-exposure modeling (predictive index), NOT ex-post causal measurement. LIMITS: measures POTENTIAL exposure, not realized labor-market outcomes; lower methodological tier for causal claims but rare cross-country coverage.
