Tier A — randomized controlled trial (legal profession). Lines: L1, L4, L7.

DESIGN/SAMPLE: RCT, law-school students randomly assigned to complete realistic legal
tasks (drafting a complaint, a client memo, etc.) with or without GPT-4; time tracked,
output blind-graded.

VERBATIM QUOTES:
- "We conducted the first randomized controlled trial to study the effect of AI
   assistance on human legal analysis. We randomly assigned law school students to
   complete realistic legal tasks either with or without the assistance of GPT-4"
- "AI assistance only slightly and inconsistently improved the quality of participants'
   legal analysis but induced large and consistent increases in speed."
- "AI assistance improved the quality of output unevenly—where it was useful at all, the
   lowest-skilled participants saw the largest improvements."
- "Because AI tools have an equalizing effect on performance, they may also promote
   equality in a famously unequal profession."

EFFECT + DIRECTION: Large, consistent SPEED gains; only slight/inconsistent QUALITY gains;
quality gains concentrated in lowest-skilled → EQUALIZING at the individual-output level
(mirrors S1/S2/S3). AUGMENT. First-hand evidence in the LEGAL occupation (previously absent).
LIMIT: law students not practicing lawyers; toy tasks; task-level not labor-market outcome;
GPT-4 (pre-reasoning-model) — see S27 for the frontier update.
