S22 — The Society of Authors (UK), "SoA AI Survey 2024" (fieldwork Jan 2024; reported April 2024)
URL: https://europeanwriterscouncil.eu/soa-survey-uk-ai-2024/  (also thebookseller.com coverage)
Facet/Line: L4-adjacent (SPECIFIC POPULATIONS: creative professions — translators, illustrators, writers),
L2/L3 (self-reported work & income loss)
Tier: D (member survey; self-report, non-random response — self-selection & salience bias)

DESIGN/SAMPLE: Survey of the Society of Authors' 12,500 members and other authors throughout January 2024;
787 responses on experiences of and concerns about generative AI.
EFFECT + DIRECTION: NEGATIVE (self-reported) — a large minority of translators/illustrators report already
losing work and income to GenAI; a distinct SPECIFIC-GROUP signal for creative occupations.

VERBATIM QUOTES:
- "A quarter of illustrators (26%) and over a third of translators (36%) have already lost work due to
  generative AI."
- "Over a third of illustrators (37%) and over 4 in 10 translators (43%) say the income from their work has
  decreased in value because of generative AI."
- "Around 1 in 10 translators (8%) and a smaller proportion of illustrators (5%) said they have used
  generative AI in their work because their publisher or commissioning organisation asked them to."
- Forward-looking: "over three quarters of translators (77%) and illustrators (78%)" expect GenAI to
  negatively impact future income.

LIMITS: LOWEST tier — self-report, ~6% response rate, advocacy-org survey (respondents likely those already
harmed/concerned → upward bias). NOT a causal or payroll estimate. But it's convergent, occupation-specific
color that MATCHES the harder Tier-B translation finding in S23 and the freelancer demand drops in S10.
Treat as testimony, not measurement.
