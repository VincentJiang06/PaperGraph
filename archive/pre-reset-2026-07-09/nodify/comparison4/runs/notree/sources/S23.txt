S23 — Dandan Qiao, Huaxia Rui & Qian Xiong, "AI and Freelancers: Has the Inflection Point Arrived?"
(Proceedings of the 58th Hawaii International Conference on System Sciences, HICSS 2025)
URL: https://gwern.net/doc/economics/automation/2024-qiao.pdf
Facet/Line: L2 (demand), L3 (earnings), L7 (polarize/uneven), + SPECIFIC OCCUPATIONS (translators vs web devs)
Tier: B (Difference-in-Differences on worker-level online-labor-market transaction data around ChatGPT launch)

DESIGN/SAMPLE: Two-way fixed-effects DiD around ChatGPT's launch, window 6 months before / 10 months after
(May 1 2022 – Oct 31 2023). Treatment occupations: translation & localization OLM (~7,582 workers) and web
development OLM (~15,000 workers); CONTROL = construction-design OLM (little exposed). Worker-level focal-job
counts & earnings.
EFFECT + DIRECTION: OPPOSITE signs by occupation — translation DISPLACED (work & earnings DOWN); web
development shows PRODUCTIVITY effect (work & earnings UP). Introduces an occupation-specific "inflection point."

VERBATIM QUOTES:
- Abstract: "we discovered two distinct scenarios following ChatGPT's launch: 1) displacement effects in
  translation & localization OLM, reducing freelancers' work volume and earnings; 2) productivity effects in
  web development OLM, increasing freelancers' work volume and earnings."
- Translation: coefficient "for log(Fjobearn) is negative and statistically significant ... suggesting a
  decrease in worker's earnings from focal jobs by 29.7% (=1−e^-0.353). These results indicate that ChatGPT
  ... [exerts] displacement effects on workers in this sector."
- Mechanism: "an inflection point exists for each occupation. Before this point, human workers benefit from
  AI enhancements; beyond this point, human workers would be replaced."
- Cites Demirci et al. (2023): "a 21% decline in job postings for writing and coding jobs" post-ChatGPT.

LIMITS: Online-labor-market only (gig, not employment); short 10-month post-window; large translation-earnings
drop (-29.7%) is FOCAL-job earnings on one platform, not total income. But it is the KEY nuance vs a blanket
"AI helps/hurts freelancers": SAME technology → occupation-specific DISPLACEMENT (translation) AND
COMPLEMENT/DEMAND-GROWTH (web dev). Distinct from S10 (Hui/Upwork): different platforms, adds the web-dev
job-CREATION-of-demand case (partial answer to GAP 4). Strengthens L7 (uneven) and specific-groups.
